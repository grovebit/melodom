# Changelog

What changed in each Melodom release, newest first. Each release's downloads and checksums are on its page under Releases.

## 0.17.0 (2026-11-25)

- **Sessions open at once while their plug-ins load:**
  - With Background Plug-in Loading (Setup › Add-ons, ticked from the start), a session's windows and transport answer as soon as it opens, while its plug-ins load behind it in their helper processes. A panel at the lower right counts "Loading plug-ins… n of m" with each plug-in's name.
  - A track stays silent until its plug-ins are in, so it never plays without its effects. A loading plug-in's window says it's still loading, and changes made meanwhile, a preset included, apply once it's in.
  - Saving while plug-ins load keeps each loading plug-in's settings exactly as they were. A bounce, or another render that uses playback, started while they load asks you to try again once they're in.
  - Untick the add-on and opening waits for every plug-in, as in Pro Tools.
  - Plug-ins also load faster: plug-ins from several makers start and load side by side, the first insert of a plug-in loads it once instead of twice, and a spare plug-in helper is started ahead of time, so the first plug-in from a maker not used yet opens sooner.
- **Batch Rename, as in Pro Tools 2026.4:**
  - Batch Track Rename renames the selected tracks: Batch Rename… on the track name menu, or Option+Shift+R. Batch Clip Rename renames the clips and whole files picked in the Clip List: Batch Rename… in the Clip List menu or on a clip's right-click menu, or Control+Shift+R.
  - The dialog has Pro Tools' sections, applied in its order: Replace (with Clear Existing Name, and Regular Expressions in Pro Tools' replace syntax), Trim, Add (Prefix, Insert At Index, Suffix) and Numbering (Position, Starting Number, Number of Places, Increment, Separate With, Use A..Z). A line under them names the first item's old and new names, or says why OK can't rename. Presets 1 to 5 recall settings (Command-click one to store), and the dialog opens with the settings it had last.
  - OK renames everything in one undo step, and each track's playlist follows its new name. Track names stay unique: a batch that would give two tracks one name is refused, and the dialog says why.
  - For clips, Process Order numbers them in Timeline Order or Clip List Sort Order, and Clip and Disk File renames whole files' audio files too (greyed out unless every picked clip is a whole file).
- **Pinned Tracks, as in Pro Tools:**
  - Pin, Pin and Replace and Unpin are on the track name menu and a group's menu. Pinned tracks show in the Pinned Tracks pane at the top of the Edit window's track area, in track order, and stay put while the tracks under them scroll.
  - A pinned row has its own view, height and lanes, and an edit made in the pane happens in the pinned row's view.
  - The pane fits its tracks until you drag its border. View › Edit Window Views › Pinned Tracks hides it, and Preferences › Display › Pinned Tracks has Auto-Adjust Pinned Area for Tracks, Adjusting Pinned Area Resizes Tracks and While Pinned Set Original Tracks Height To.
  - The pane's Search shows the pinned tracks as tags (a tag's X unpins it) and finds tracks to pin. Command-Option-click on a group in the Groups list pins its tracks in place of the pinned ones; with Shift, it adds them.
- **Track › Duplicate copies a folder's tracks:** the dialog has Pro Tools' All Member Tracks, Elastic Audio algorithm and ARA plugin assignment, all ticked to start and remembered. A folder's copy holds copies of its tracks, nested folders too, each taking the dialog's other choices, in one undo step; a copied Routing Folder gets a bus of its own, and the copied tracks go to it. With Elastic Audio algorithm unticked the copy has no Elastic Audio and its clips play unwarped; with ARA plugin assignment unticked it has no ARA plug-in.
- **Notes view:**
  - As in Pro Tools, a Command+Option+Control drag up or down on the mini-keyboard scrolls the rows, and so does Command+Option+Control with the wheel over the notes. The keyboard runs the track's full height, without scroll arrows.
  - Edit › Separate (Command+E) cuts the notes at the Edit cursor or the selection's ends and leaves the clip whole, and Edit › Consolidate (Option+Shift+3) joins the picked notes, or those in the Edit selection, as in Pro Tools.
  - Notes moved or copied past a MIDI clip's end grow the clip out to the bar line after them, and past its start back to the bar line before them, up to the clips beside it, in Notes view and the MIDI Editor alike, as in Pro Tools. An edit-locked clip keeps its bounds.
- **The Edit window looks more like Pro Tools':**
  - A vertical zoom scale beside every track: dB marks on volume and send views and lanes, and amplitude marks for each channel on waveforms. Right-click it for Track Height.
  - Clip names are labels over the clip, so waveforms, notes, the line between channels, the zoom scale and Notes view's keyboard use the whole track height. Video thumbnails fill the track too.
  - In Dark, lanes are Pro Tools' lighter grey, each ending in a rim and a rule. Grid lines are lighter and Slip lines darker than the lanes, the selection matches Pro Tools', and automation lines are lighter, to read better.
  - Edit modes light in Pro Tools' colours, in the Edit window and the MIDI Editor: Shuffle red, Spot amber, Slip green, Grid blue and Relative Grid purple. Snap to Grid's outline is Grid's blue.
  - Tracks set to a height between Small and Medium pack their header's keys in one row at Pro Tools' sizes, with the Elastic Audio/ARA field on audio tracks and Patch Select on MIDI and Instrument tracks. Pan views mark L, R and the centre, and a stereo track's pan views read "panleft" and "pnrght" on small keys.
  - Selector names (I/O, sends, groups and automation) are written as in Pro Tools: centred when they fit, else from the left, cut by whole letters with no "…". A long input name no longer loses its first letter.
  - The Ruler View selector has a check box per ruler, Key and Chords included, in Pro Tools' three groups; Tempo Editor, All Marker Rulers, All and Minimal stay in View › Rulers. The MIDI Editor's ruler menu has check boxes too.
  - The Tracks list's header has no +, and a Master Fader has no Input selector.
- **Automation reads as in Pro Tools':** a faint tint in the track's colour under every automation line, Trim included, fainter while suspended, in the Edit window's rows and lanes and the MIDI Editor's lanes. Lines are 2 points wide, and the timeline no longer writes the parameter's name; the view selector and lane headers keep it.
- **Volume and pan in the Edit window's I/O column:** as in Pro Tools, click a track's volume for a pop-up fader with a level you can type, or its pan for a pan slider, one a side on stereo tracks. A MIDI track's set MIDI Volume and MIDI Pan. Option-click resets and Command-drag moves finely on these, on the plug-in sliders Melodom draws (plug-in windows, Clip Effects and AudioSuite) and on Strip Silence's sliders.
- **The MIDI Keyboard window:** keys light green while their note sounds, from the pointer or a computer key. While it's open, the computer keys play it whichever window is in front, as in Pro Tools; text fields and dialogs keep their keys, and held notes end when it closes. A held velocity key keeps stepping. It keeps its octave and velocity between launches, and says "MIDI is unavailable" when there's no MIDI system. MIDI and Instrument tracks' Mix strips have Pro Tools' Patch Select button beside the track type, with an icon where the program's name won't fit.
- **The track name menu is Pro Tools':** its items come in Pro Tools' order and sections, with Melodom's add-ons last. Control-click opens it, and Control-Shift-click scrolls the track into view. Track Height moved to the round arrow left of the name.
- **Edit-locked clips ask first:** as in Pro Tools, an edit that would change an edit-locked clip asks Cancel or Allow: Separate, Clear, Cut, Paste, trims, Consolidate, Strip Silence, Nudge, clip gain, the Separation Grabber, the TCE Trim tool, Warp and Event markers and loops, and imports and AudioSuite before their work starts. Nudge leaves time-locked clips and the selection where they are, and Separate and Nudge say why when nothing happened.
- **Melodom says what went wrong:**
  - A failed command is named by its menu item ("Can't Undo: nothing to undo"), greyed menu items say why on hover, track name and clip menus included, and Undo and Redo name the step they took ("Undid Mute").
  - Record tries again an input that failed to open; if none opens, a notice that stays says to choose one in Setup › Playback Engine. Input lost to a slow disk, a recording that stopped and a take that couldn't be written leave notices that stay.
  - A take that heard only silence says Melodom may not have microphone access, and where to allow it. A failed Auto Backup says so once, naming the Backups folder.
- **The Dashboard:**
  - Right-click a recent session for Open, Remove from Recent List, Show in Finder and Clear Recent List, as in Pro Tools.
  - Recent sessions and the New page's location are checked in the background, one path at a time, so a disconnected network drive no longer holds up the list or keeps every row at "checking…".
  - Session Configuration has Bit Depth (Command+B steps it, as Command+R steps the rate), and new sessions start at the rate and depth you used last.
  - When a session can't be created, the reason leads with what to do. A failed update check offers Try Again and Open Release Page.
- **Dialogs and floating windows:** Return presses a dialog's default button only when freshly pressed: not the Return that opened the dialog, not a held key's repeats, and not while a checkbox or pop-up has the focus, where it acts on that control. Recover Unsaved Work is a Pro Tools-style dialog: Return recovers, Not Now keeps the work for next time, and Discard… asks first. Window › Hide All Floating Windows (Control+Option+Command+W) shows again what it hid, as in Pro Tools. Output and Send windows close with it, and when another session opens.
- **Imported sound starts on time:** sound from M4A and MP4 files and movies started about 44 ms late, with the encoder's padding at its end, and AAC sound in Matroska files about 21 ms late; both now start where they should. A session that still uses an M4A or MP4 file itself as a source (rare: saving turns such files into WAV) opens with those clips' sound that much earlier. A MIDI file with stray bytes after its last track imports, and a damaged one imports what can be read, with a warning that names what was lost and stays until you close it.
- **Smaller changes:**
  - The Workspace tags several files in the background, with progress and Cancel, and its Key menu spells keys as the Key column does (C#m, F#m, G#m). Assign Tags leaves alone the session's audio files it reaches after a recording starts.
  - Strip Silence cuts a playlist in one pass, so long takes with thousands of clips no longer hold up the app.
  - VST3 plug-ins in /Network/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 are found.
  - Clip Effects' EQ and Dynamics sliders follow their parameters' taper.
  - Preferences › Operation greys out "Keep N most recent backups" while Auto Backup is off.
- **Fixes:** AudioSuite on a whole clip, and Elastic Audio, TCE and Warp renders, keep the crossfade with the clip beside it, where it used to dip. Volume, Volume Trim and VCA Master automation is read at every sample, so playback and bounces sound the same at any buffer size. Automating a plug-in between extreme values no longer sends it an infinite value. Crash recovery repairs only takes that are exactly Melodom's own, and exports and saves never write through a link to another file. Damaged audio and movie files can no longer use up memory, and a movie whose path isn't a regular file no longer hangs the video.
- **For agents and `melodom-cli`:**
  - `track.batch_rename` and `clip.batch_rename`; `track.pin` (Pin, Pin and Replace, Unpin) and `view.pinned_height`; `track.duplicate` takes `members`, `elastic` and `ara`.
  - `allow_locked` lets a command change edit-locked clips; `edit.undo` and `edit.redo` reply with the step's `label`; `file.import_midi` replies `skipped` and `summary`; `mix.volume` replies `volumes` and `clamped`; `ui.key` and `ui.click` take `hold` (`down` or `up`).

## 0.16.0 (2026-11-22)

- **Clip groups work as Pro Tools' do:**
  - A group acts as one clip: picking, moving, nudging, shifting or copying any of its clips takes the whole group, and so does a click in its empty space. Group takes the selection's range, across tracks and into empty space, cutting clips at its edges.
  - It's drawn as one clip across its tracks, with its name and the Clip Group icon. The icon shows audio, MIDI or both, and a break when the group is separated (a track moved between its tracks or away, hidden or deleted); Regroup makes it whole again.
  - The Trim tool trims a group by its outer edges. Clips past the new edge go out of sight, and come back when the group is trimmed out again.
  - Groups nest. Group on a selection holding groups makes a group of them; Ungroup takes off the outer layer, Ungroup All every layer, and Regroup puts back what was taken off. Regrouping a group used more than once asks Change All: Modify makes every other use match it, Copy makes it a group of its own.
  - Rename, Mute, Edit Lock, Time Lock and Quantize to Grid act on the whole group, and Group and Ungroup are on the right-click menu. Separate inside a group makes two groups, as on a clip.
  - A group has a clip gain level and line of its own, played on top of its clips' own gain. Ungroup takes the group's gain and the fades on its outer edges off, and Regroup puts them back. Tab stops at a group's edges.
  - A group on tick-based tracks keeps its bars and beats through tempo changes. Playlists view takes a track's groups apart first, as Pro Tools has none there.
  - The Clip List lists each group with the Clip Group icon: click it to pick the group, or drag it onto a track for a copy. The Clip List menu's Show picks Audio, MIDI, Video and Groups.
  - File › Import › Clip Groups asks where the groups go, as Pro Tools does: onto new tracks (at the session start, the song start, the selection or where the Spot dialog says) or into the Clip List, and whether to take the file's tempo map. Export Clip Groups… writes a file for each group into the session's Clip Groups folder, and asks about each file that's already there. Clip group files dropped from the Finder or the Workspace land where you drop them.
  - Clip group files keep the groups' names, nesting and clip gain, and the tempo map. They're Melodom's own files (.scgrp): Pro Tools' .cgrp files don't open.
- **MIDI to outboard gear, and where an Instrument track's MIDI goes:**
  - A MIDI track's MIDI Output selector picks a channel of your Mac's MIDI output ports, as in Pro Tools, so the track plays outboard synths in time with what you hear, MIDI Track Offsets included. Muting, soloing, locating, Stop and All MIDI Notes Off end its notes. Ports open when they're plugged in, and a session keeps a port that isn't.
  - An Instrument track's MIDI Output picks where its MIDI goes: nowhere, a port's channel, its own MIDI Chain (Chain In) or another Instrument track's, or plug-ins on any track's inserts (Individual Targets). Clicking it opens that menu, as in Pro Tools, instead of the instrument. Ports are listed last, as Pro Tools lists its devices.
  - A MIDI track's fader and pan are MIDI Volume (0 to 127, controller 7) and MIDI Pan (<64 to 63>, controller 10), as in Pro Tools, sent wherever its MIDI goes. The fader follows the clips' own controller events, which the Volume and Pan views show, and automation passes in Touch, Latch and Write write them into the clip under the playhead. Instrument tracks have MIDI Volume and Pan in the Instruments view, and MIDI faders are marked 127, 96, 64, 32 and 0.
- **The MIDI Keyboard window plays like a keyboard:** Window › MIDI Keyboard is a MIDI device of its own (MIDI Keyboard Window), heard by tracks whose MIDI Input is All, and a held key sounds until you let go. While it's in front, the computer keys play it, as in Pro Tools: A to K and W, E, T, Y and U the notes, Z and X the octave, C and V the velocity, with the letters on its keys. It enters notes only while Step Input is on.
- **Instruments with more than one output:** an Aux Input's Input selector lists, by track, the outputs past the main one of the session's instruments (CLAP, VST3 and Audio Units), named as the plug-ins name them, as in Pro Tools. The Aux plays that output in time with the buses, and the main output stays on the instrument's track.
- **Mono and stereo inserts:** on a mono track, the Insert selector lists each plug-in's (mono) version, and its (mono/stereo) version where it has one. From such an insert on, the track is stereo, with two panners, stereo sends and a stereo meter; take the insert off and it's mono again. Commit, Track Bounce and Freeze keep it stereo.
- **Multi-mono plug-ins:** on a stereo or wider track, the Insert selector offers each effect's (multi-mono) version, built-in or hosted (VST3, Audio Units and CLAP): a mono copy for each channel. Master Link starts on, as in Pro Tools; with it off, the plug-in window's Channel selector shows and edits each channel's own settings, and Link Enable links channels again. A channel bypassed on its own turns the insert purple.
- **Bounce Mix and Track Bounce:**
  - Both offer Mono (Summed) and Pad to Frame Boundary, as in Pro Tools, and Bounce Mix writes WAV, AIFF and FLAC at any sample rate.
  - Bounce Mix's Mix Source lists output paths, buses and physical outputs, with Search, as in Pro Tools. It bounces up to 16 sources at once, a file each named after its source, as Pro Tools Studio and Ultimate do: the plus and minus buttons add and take off rows, and a row's grip drags it.
- **Takes are recorded at the session's bit depth** (16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float), as Broadcast WAVs stamped with where on the session's timecode the recording began, as in Pro Tools. Recovery after a crash repairs them at every depth.
- **The Smart Tool makes crossfades:** at the bottom of the cut between two touching audio clips the Crossfade cursor shows, and a drag left or right makes a crossfade centred on the cut with the Default Fade Settings, as in Pro Tools.
- **Option for every track, Option+Shift for the selected ones,** as in Pro Tools, on the Automation Mode selector, on a playlist picked, made or duplicated in the Playlist selector, and on the MIDI Input selector. Option-click on any meter clears every meter, as Option+C and Clear All Clip Indicators now do, peaks included. Control-click on an insert bypasses it and the inserts below it, and on a send mutes it and the sends below it; with Option, on every track.
- **Insert Time and Cut Time move the tempo and meter rulers with the music,** as in Pro Tools, and playlists not in use follow the moved tempo. MIDI on tick-based tracks lands on its exact tick, so a note at a moved clip's start no longer goes silent.
- **Setup › Add-ons** lists Melodom's features that Pro Tools doesn't have (the FMOD Studio link, Remote Control, the Import Audio waveform, and note Paste that keeps other pitches), each with a tick. Untick them, or click Pro Tools Only, for Pro Tools alone; sessions keep what they were saved with. Remote Control moved here from Preferences › Operation, keeping your choice.
- **The Clip List picks several:** Command-click and Shift-click pick several rows, and a drag places them all in one undo step, Top to Bottom from the track you drop on, then on new tracks. Clip List › Timeline Drop Order switches to Left to Right, end to end on one track, as in Pro Tools. Spot spots the first, Audio to MIDI takes them all on an Instrument track, and a refused drop says why.
- **Smaller changes:**
  - Menus tick the current choice everywhere.
  - Bounce… is on the track name menu, and Track Bounce starts in the open session's folder.
  - A send made through "new track…" opens its Send window.
  - Import Session Data from a Pro Tools session or an AAF offers only what it brings.
  - Quantize settings are saved with the session, and Step Input's are kept between launches.
  - Help › Melodom Guide and Help › Keyboard Shortcuts.
  - Return closes update notices that only tell you something.
  - Long Grid and Nudge menus scroll.
- **Fixes:** plug-in windows' Presets button no longer shows a box where its arrow was. Built-in plug-ins no longer show "-0" for cents, semitones and percentages just below zero. The file name shown while you drag from the Workspace reads in both themes.
- **For agents and `melodom-cli`:**
  - `file.bounce_mix` takes a `source` (a bus, an output path or physical outputs) or up to 16 `sources`, a file each, and `file_format: mono_summed`, `sample_rate` and `pad_to_frame`.
  - Clip groups: `clip.trim_group`, `clip.group_gain_line`, `clip.held_place`, `clip.held_rename` and `clip.held_clear` (groups held in the Clip List), and `change_all` (`modify` or `copy`) on `clip.regroup`.
  - `mix.midi_volume` and `mix.midi_pan`; `mix.insert_link` for a multi-mono insert's Master Link and Link Enable; `track.view_playlists`.

## 0.15.0 (2026-11-12)

- **Menus look like Pro Tools 2026's:** the menu bar's, right-click and pop-up menus have square corners, even rows with Pro Tools' check mark in a column of its own, thin arrows on submenus, heavier separators and a full-width highlight. In the Light theme they take Pro Tools' Classic greys. A menu opened near the window's edge moves to fit, and a right-click menu taller than the window scrolls. The Edit window's right-click shows ARA Plugins greyed out where it doesn't apply, as Pro Tools does.
- **File › Import › Audio (Command+Shift+I), laid out like Pro Tools 2026's:**
  - A sidebar: Favorites (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Music and your own), the session's folder, Recent, and Locations (your home folder and disks). Right-click a favorite to remove it.
  - Back and Forward, and a pop-up of the folders the shown one is in, with Choose Folder… and Add to Favorites.
  - Name, Length, Size, Kind and Date Modified columns. Click a column's title to sort by it, and again to reverse; the sort is remembered.
  - Pro Tools' options below the list: the file's type, length, size, bit depth, sample rate and channels; Clips in Current File; Add, Add All, Copy and Copy All (Convert and Convert All when a file can't play where it is); Clips to Import, with Remove and Remove All; a Start/End/Length box; the preview's Stop and Play, a position slider, Loop, Auto Play, the meter and the level; and Apply SRC with the file's and the session's sample rates. The waveform, which Pro Tools doesn't show, sits under Clips in Current File: click in it to play from there.
  - New Folder, Hide Options (remembered), Cancel and Open. Drag the title row to move the dialog; Esc cancels.
  - The search, Subfolders, files dropped on the dialog, its keys and the choice of where the files go work as before.
- **Double-clicks go at your Mac's speed** everywhere in Melodom: a slower double-click no longer reads as two clicks, and a double-click soon after a click no longer counts as a triple-click. In the Workspace, that sometimes kept a double-click from opening a folder.
- **Sound Library locations:**
  - A folder inside another location can be added as a location of its own. Its files are listed, searched and counted in Soundbase's tags once.
  - A location whose folder is gone, or on a disk that isn't connected, is listed in red and can be removed.
  - Add Sound Library Location opens Sound Libraries in the Locations pane, so the new location shows at once.
  - A Sound Libraries folder whose name ends in a space is no longer misread.
- **Y moves clips to the current timecode,** as Pro Tools' Move Clip Start To Current Timecode does: the picked clips' start goes to the incoming MIDI Time Code, or to the session start when none comes in, and several clips keep their spacing. Press Y with Commands Keyboard Focus on, or Control+Y with it off. It's one undo step.
- **Fixes:** inside a selection on an automation lane, the Smart Tool keeps its zones, as in Pro Tools: a drag in the lane's top quarter trims the automation, lower down it makes a new selection, and near a breakpoint it moves that breakpoint. Before, a drag anywhere inside the selection trimmed it.
- **For agents and `melodom-cli`:** `edit.move_clip_start_to_timecode` moves the picked clips' start to `at`, else to the incoming time code, else to the session start.

## 0.14.0 (2026-11-11)

- **Melodom starts on the Dashboard,** as Pro Tools does: at launch it's all Melodom shows, in a window of its own size, until you make or open a session; then the window goes back to the size you left it at. Quit (Command+Q) takes Cancel's place, and until then the menus offer only New, Open Session, Dashboard and Quit. File › Close Session brings the Dashboard back too (Preferences › Display › Show Dashboard After Closing a Session), and so does Discard on the offer to recover unsaved work after a crash.
- **Soundbase and the Workspace, more like Pro Tools:**
  - A Waveform column, first after Name: each audio file's waveform beside its Preview button. Click in a waveform to play the file from that point, and press Command+Option+] or [ to draw the waveforms taller or back. Melodom reads the waveforms in the background and keeps them, so they show at once the next time.
  - Advanced Search (Command+Shift+F, or the Advanced button): up to eight rows, each a column, a criterion and what to look for, such as Key Is Am or Tempo Is Greater Than 120. Soundbase opens with Pro Tools Intro's rows (Kind Is Audio File; Key, Time Signature and Loop Is (any); Any Text Column Contains), which narrow the list once you change one or press Return in one. Command+Option-click a column's title to add a row for it, and Shift+Plus and Shift+Minus add a row and take the last one off. With Subfolders on, the folder view's rows look in the folders inside too.
  - Soundbase lists your Sound Libraries' folders too, after their files, as Pro Tools does.
  - The search field reads its words as Pro Tools does: words apart find names with any of them, and words in quotes find names with all of them. Import Audio's search works the same way.
  - The Workspace opens larger, so every column and the Advanced Search rows fit.
- **What's New** shows only this version's changes. Every release's are in CHANGELOG.md on GitHub.
- **Changed:** the About window no longer has a Contributors tab.
- **Fixes:** with Kontakt 8 or another plug-in built with Qt open, a second Melodom no longer appears in the Dock.
- **For agents and `melodom-cli`:** while the Dashboard is all Melodom shows (a launch from the Finder, or after `session.close`), the control channel and MCP run only `session.new`, `session.open` and `app.quit`; anything else answers that no session is open.

## 0.13.0 (2026-11-10)

- **Soundbase, after Pro Tools' sample browser:**
  - Window › New Workspace › Soundbase (Option+Control+I) searches all of your Sound Libraries at once, as "Workspace: Search In Sound Libraries", with the number of results.
  - The Tags pane shows every tag as a button, most-used first. Click a tag to include it, Option-click to leave it out; chosen tags show as chips in the Search Tags field, and Esc clears them. Control+Option+Command+T shows or hides the pane, and the heart (Control+Option+Command+F) shows only your favourites.
  - Tempo, Key, Time Signature, Loop, Favorite, Rating and Tags columns, read from WAV, AIFF and MIDI files as Pro Tools reads them, so Pro Tools' own loops show their tags, keys and tempos. Loops show their length in bars and beats.
  - Assign Tags (Option+Control+T, right-click › Tags…, or a click in the Tags cell) adds and removes tags, suggests the ones you already use, and sets the heart, 0–5 stars and Loop (Control+F, Control+0–5 and Control+O while it's open). The Key, Time Signature, Loop, Rating and Favorite cells edit with a click. An edit applies to every picked file, and tagging several files asks first. Right-click › Recently Used Tags adds a tag you used lately.
  - Tags, key, time signature and loop are written into the file where Pro Tools reads them, without moving its audio. Favourites and ratings stay in Melodom's own settings, as Pro Tools keeps them in its database. A file Melodom can't tag safely, such as a read-only one, is left alone and the reason shown.
  - Not yet: the waveform column, Preview in Context, catalogs and Advanced Search.
- **The Workspace:**
  - A Locations pane with Sound Libraries: Pro Tools' own Sound Libraries is linked in automatically, and any folder can be added from its right-click.
  - Columns sort from their titles (click again to reverse, Shift-click for up to four levels) and show or hide from a title's right-click or Option-click.
- **What's new, in the app:** after Melodom updates itself, it shows once what changed in the new version, and Help › What's New in Melodom shows every release's changes any time. From the next update on, the update offer also lists the new version's changes before you click Restart Now.
- **More like Pro Tools:**
  - Left and Right arrows scroll the Edit window to the selection's start or end, or to the insertion point.
  - F6+F7 and F7+F8 choose the Smart Tool, and Command+6 and Command+7 the Pencil and the Smart Tool.
  - Option+Shift+E and an Option-click cancel Zoom Toggle and keep the view, and a zoom key held down keeps zooming.
  - The Sub counter has Pro Tools' Sub Counter selector, and setting the Main counter to the Sub counter's format switches the two.
  - Control+N turns Insertion Follows Playback on and off.
  - A new send opens its Send window.
  - Double-clicking a selected track's colour strip keeps the selection for the Color Palette, and a track name that's cut off shows whole when you point at it.
  - Link Track and Edit Selection has a toolbar icon of its own.
  - Timecode and Feet+Frames rulers, grids and Slip lines mark the counter's whole values and line up with the counters; drop-frame seconds land on labelled seconds.

## 0.9.0 (2026-10-25)

- **ARA plug-ins such as Melodyne, the Pro Tools way:**
  - Track and Clip menus, and a track name's right-click menu, have a submenu for each ARA plug-in: Edit, Clear, Bypass and Render.
  - Setup › Preferences › Editing › Double-Clicking on Audio Clip Opens: the Name dialog, the clip's ARA editor or its Clip Effects editor.
  - View › Clip › ARA Note Overlay shows the notes the plug-in found, and a clip's ARA icon fills while the plug-in analyses it.
  - Commit, Freeze, Track Bounce, Consolidate, Prepare DPE Tracks, AudioSuite, Render Clip Gain and Clip Effects, and Export Clips as Files all hear the plug-in's edits. While a render runs, ARA windows step aside so no edit lands in it.
  - `melodom-cli` runs ARA plug-ins too: its bounces and renders hear the edits, and its saves keep them.
- **Renders and imports on disk:** Commit, Freeze, Consolidate, Render Clip Gain and Clip Effects, AudioSuite and Elastic warps write their audio into files where takes go, and play it from there. Importing audio (File › Import › Audio, dropping files, opening from the Finder) converts on its own thread behind a progress window.
- **Audio interfaces:** opening the output sets the interface to the session's sample rate, as Pro Tools does. An input on another interface than the output stays in time: macOS joins them in a private aggregate device, as Pro Tools' Aggregate I/O does. JACK is listed while a JACK server runs.
- **When something fails, Melodom says why:** a launch that fails shows an alert with where the log is, and a plug-in that can't be set up says why in the status line instead of passing its input through silently.
- **Nothing half-done:** a command that fails leaves nothing of itself behind, a take writer that hits a problem keeps its takes and says so, finished takes are synced to the disk, and a punch that keeps nothing leaves the session untouched.
- **Steadier mixing:** a plug-in taken out for putting out invalid audio keeps its latency, so delay compensation stays put, and long sessions no longer grow in memory with every fader move.
- **Smaller downloads:** the app and `melodom-cli` ship without their debug symbols, optimised as a whole.
